2. What the app is for

SmartClass Teacher AI helps teachers prepare lesson notes, schemes of work, questions, worksheets, marking suggestions and report card comments, and to keep simple class records (class lists, attendance and scores). It is made for teachers and other adults working in education. It is not meant for children.

4. How we use your information

  • To run the app and produce the notes, questions, worksheets, marks and comments you ask for.
  • To keep your library and class records so you can come back to them.
  • To apply free and paid plan limits and to manage subscriptions.
  • To keep the service secure, stop misuse, and fix errors.
  • To reply when you contact us for support.
  • To meet our legal duties.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not show advertising, and we do not use your content to build advertising profiles.

5. AI processing

To create a result, the text you enter in a tool is sent securely to our AI service provider, Anthropic, which processes it and sends the result back. We send only what is needed for that request, such as the subject, class, topic, your question and marking guide, the student answers you paste, and, for report comments, the pupil names, scores and notes in the form. We do not send your password, email address or phone number. If you use "Load from my class", the names, scores and attendance notes loaded into the form are sent when you tap Generate. You can read the provider's privacy information at anthropic.com/privacy.

If you use Read it from a photo when adding a textbook, the photo or photos of the contents page are sent to the same AI provider to read the text. We do not keep the photos. Only the text you save is stored.

Our advice: use pupils' first names or initials where you can, and do not type sensitive details such as health, family or disciplinary matters into the tools. AI results can contain mistakes, so please check them before you use them.

6. Who we share information with

  • Service providers that help us run the app: our hosting provider (Render) and our AI provider (Anthropic).
  • Google Play, when you buy a subscription through it. Google handles your payment details. We do not see your card number.
  • Authorities or courts, only when the law requires it.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is ever sold or merged, and then only with the same protections for your information.

These providers may only use your information to provide their service to us.

7. Pupils' information and your responsibility

When you add pupils' names, attendance or scores, you are entering information about children. Please make sure your school permits you to keep those records in this app. We use that information only to provide the features to you, and we do not use it for any other purpose. The app is not directed at children, and children should not create accounts. If you believe a child has created an account, please email us and we will delete it.

8. Where your information is processed

Our hosting and AI providers may process information on servers outside Nigeria. When that happens, we rely on those providers' security and data-protection commitments, and we handle your information in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

9. How long we keep your information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account details, library, classroom records and usage records from our live systems straight away. Any backup copies are removed within 30 days. Aggregated figures that cannot identify you may be kept.

10. Deleting your account and data

  • In the app: open Account, scroll to Delete my account, enter your password and confirm.
  • On the web: go to our account deletion page.
  • By email: write to support@smartclassaiacademy.com from the email address on your account, or give us the phone number on your account.

You can also delete a single saved item, pupil, class or assessment inside the app at any time.

11. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access controls so each teacher can see only their own data. No online service can be perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password and keep it private.

12. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have the right to ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and withdraw consent where we rely on it. You can do most of these inside the app. For anything else, email support@smartclassaiacademy.com. We will reply within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make an important change, we will tell you in the app or by email.
