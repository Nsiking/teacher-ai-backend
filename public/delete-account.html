Delete my account

SmartClass Teacher AI · Last updated: 4 November 2026

You can delete your SmartClass Teacher AI account and all the data linked to it. This page explains how.

What gets deleted

  • Your account details (name, email, phone number and password).
  • Your whole library: lesson notes, schemes of work, questions, worksheets, marking results and report comments.
  • Your classroom record: classes, pupils, attendance, assessments and scores.
  • Your usage records.

The deletion takes effect straight away and cannot be undone. Backup copies, if any, are removed within 30 days. If you have a paid plan through Google Play, deleting your account does not cancel the subscription. Please cancel it in Google Play as well.

Option 1: Delete inside the app

Open the app, tap Account, scroll down to Delete my account, enter your password and confirm.

Option 2: Delete here

Your account has been deleted.

Your data has been removed. Thank you for using SmartClass Teacher AI. Remember to cancel any Google Play subscription if you have one.

Option 3: Ask us by email

If you forgot your password or can no longer open the app, email support@smartclassaiacademy.com from the email address on your account, or tell us the phone number on your account. Write "Delete my account" in the subject. We will confirm that the account is yours, delete it, and aim to do this within 7 days.
